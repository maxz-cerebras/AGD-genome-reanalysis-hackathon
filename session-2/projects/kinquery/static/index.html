Limitations (read before trusting a count)
  • Cohort = the 100 probands. Relatives ("0", "1", …) are pedigree entries, not patient records; they only provide family history.
  • Age is computed from birth year only (dates are exported at year precision). 20 probands and most relatives have no dateOfBirth → “could not be assessed”, never silently dropped.
  • A genes[] entry is a finding; pathogenicity, zygosity and age at onset are not in the export and cannot be queried.
  • Phenotype matching uses HPO 2024-08-13 subsumption (the release the data was coded with). A relative's cancer recorded only as a MIM/ORDO diagnosis is a diagnosis, not a phenotype.
  • The model only writes the structured query and its restatement; every match, count and evidence line is computed from the file. Model output is validated and grounded; corrections and unresolved terms are shown, not hidden.
